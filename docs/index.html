About 3n+1

The map, the problem, and the meaning of 3n+1. From Payton (sharpninja).

What 3n+1 is

The Collatz map, also called the hailstone map, is a rule for a positive integer n.

  • If n is even, n goes to n / 2.
  • If n is odd, n goes to 3n + 1.

Iterate that step. The values form a trajectory, a hailstone: the number rises and falls. This plotter draws the value against the step. It can also play a trajectory as sound.

Each curve stops when the value reaches 1. The cycle 4 → 2 → 1 is not drawn again and again.

The problem

The Collatz conjecture says every positive integer eventually reaches the cycle 4 → 2 → 1. Equivalently, every positive integer eventually reaches 1.

The conjecture has been checked for enormous ranges of n. The mathematical Collatz conjecture remains unproven.

Observations

Stress-test seeds

Trajectories that feel hardest, or most interesting to watch, often start at a prime power with an odd exponent: pk where k is odd. That includes the primes themselves (k = 1) and cubes such as 27 = 3³. Even exponents are a milder class, such as 9 = 3² and 25 = 5².

The seed syntax oddprimepowers: and ppodd: targets that set. Try oddprimepowers:8..32.

Shared peaks

The seeds 27, 31, 41, and 47 all reach the famous peak 9,232. They do because 41, then 31, then 47 already sit on 27’s path. The first value those four seeds share is 47.

Turn on Align, or read First common value after Generate, to see where paths merge.

Meaning

Collatz is like gravity. It's inevitable, but it feeds on entropy.

Reality is recursive, and the resolution of each recursion is a moment in time. Some moments mean more, but they all mean something. Collatz is like a graph of energy over time, which is consequence.

This is literally why music must resolve. Music that doesn't resolve is dissonant. It's unfinished, and it is shocking. Just like when a life ends early and the result of consequence is left dangling. The anchor: Music always decays to nothing, regardless of resolution. Life always decays to nothing. Gravity always wins. Entropy always clusters. 3x+1 always reaches 1. The journey is the reward, not the destination. It's a sequence of tension builds, then it releases to the next tension build. There's some philosophy in here as well. The chains of rising tension followed by relief until meeting the power of 2 that marks the descent into death. It mirrors life. The tension (consequence) of a life builds through a person's work and procreation until the tipping point is hit and the life fades to oblivion. It's the cycle of free will, but only when life ends naturally. It's the repeating cycle of Für Elise. The beats of the music imitate the beats of a lifetime of consequence.
